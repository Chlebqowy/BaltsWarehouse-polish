
Vouchers:
	1. There's Options
		+1 option in each Standard, Arcana, or Planet pack
	2. More Options
		+1 option in packs not covered by There's Options
	
	1. 1-Up
		Returns to the previous shop upon death with +$20; Self destructs
	2. Resurrection
		Returns to the previous shop upon death with +$30 and a Coupon Tag; Self destructs
			Can only appear once 1-Up has triggered
	
	1. Easy Button
		x0.9 Blind size
	2. Wimpmode
		Blinds scale by the previous Ante

Tags:
	Shop Tag (All antes)
		Return to the Shop, earning Interest
	Luxury Tag (Ante 3+)
		Next Shop cannot have Common Jokers, and may have Legendary Jokers
	Shrink Tag (Ante 7+)
		x0.75 Blind Size next round
	Booster Tag (All antes)
		Next Booster Pack has +2 options

Jokers:
	Sorcerer [Uncommon]
		+10 Mult per Spectral Card used this run
	Bartender [Uncommon]
		Can discard 1 extra card per discard; -1 hand size
	Librarian [Common]
		+10 Chips per unique played card this run
	Sketch [Rare]
		Copies the ability of the last sold Joker

Enhancements:
	Hole-Punched
		+0.5 hand size when held in hand
	Worn
		1 in 2 chance to destroy this card when discarded // Meant for deck-thinning
	Dog-Eared
		Discards containing this card have a 1 in 3 chance to retain the discard
	Virtual
		Returns to the deck once played if deck has any non-Virtual cards

Editions:
	Stellar
		Played hand has a temporary extra level when held in hand (Playing Cards)
		Played hands have a temporary extra level (Jokers)
		(Added to Aura and Wheel of Fortune)

Suits:
	Blank
		Copies the rank and suit of the card to the right

Tarots:
	Strength (Inverted)
		Enhances up to 5 selected cards into Worn Cards
	Sun/Moon/Star/World (Inverted)
		Destroys up to 5 selected Hearts/Clubs/Diamonds/Spades
	Judgement (Inverted)
		Sells a selected Joker for 1.5x its buy value
	Lovers (Inverted)
		Converts 1 selected card into a Blank Card
	Magician (Inverted)
		2 in 5 chance to copy 1 selected card
	Chariot (Inverted)
		Enhances 1 selected card into a Hole-Punched Card
	Justice (Inverted)
		Enhances 1 selected card into a Dog-Eared Card
	Heirophant (Inverted)
		Enhances 1 selected card into a Virtual Card

Spectrals:
	Do-Over
		Reshuffle the deck; Reset Hands and Discards; Set Round score to 0
	Hyperdrive
		Add a Pink Seal to 1 selected card

Seals:
	Pink Seal:
		Balances 10% of Chips and Mult
